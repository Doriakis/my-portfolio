Megaline Telecom Analysis (Major EDA Project)

A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis project focusing on user behavior and revenue generation for a telecom company.

  • Revenue Calculation: Engineered complex features to calculate exact monthly revenue per user by factoring in base plan costs and writing logic to dynamically calculate penalty fees for overages on calls, texts, and data.
  • Statistical Analysis & Visualization: Calculated descriptive statistics (mean, variance, standard deviation) and built visualizations (histograms, bar charts, boxplots) using matplotlib to compare the "Surf" and "Ultimate" plans.
  • Hypothesis Testing: Formulated and executed independent two-sample Welch's t-tests using scipy.stats to mathematically prove whether revenue differed significantly between the two plans and between the NY-NJ region and the rest of the country.
